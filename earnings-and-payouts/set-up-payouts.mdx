To get paid, add a payout method on the **Money** page: **PayPal** (recommended) or a **bank account**. You only do this once. After that, we pay your balance automatically on a schedule; there's nothing to request.

## Open the payout settings

1. Go to **Money**.
2. In the **Getting paid** checklist, click **Add payout method**. The page scrolls to the **Payout method** section, which has **PayPal** and **Bank account**.

Links to payout setup elsewhere in the app, and in our emails, also open this section.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-payout-destination-dialog.webp" alt="The payout method options with the PayPal email field, the Send verification code button and the Set up a bank account button numbered" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

## Option 1: PayPal (recommended)

PayPal is the fastest way to get paid, from anywhere.

1. Under **PayPal**, enter the email address on your PayPal account in **Your PayPal email**.
2. Click **Send verification code**. We email a 6-digit code to that address.
3. Enter the code in **6-digit verification code** and click **Verify PayPal**. If it didn't arrive, check your spam folder or click **Resend code**.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-paypal-verification-code.webp" alt="The PayPal section with the verification code field and the Verify PayPal button numbered" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

The checklist now shows **Paid by PayPal** with your address.

### The 72-hour security hold
For your security, payouts to a newly added PayPal address start 72 hours after you add it. The checklist shows **Security hold on your new PayPal address** with the exact time payouts can start, and we email your login address about the change. If you didn't add the address, contact us right away.

![The Getting paid checklist showing the security hold on a new PayPal address](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-getting-paid-paypal-hold.webp)

### Changing your PayPal email
Once your PayPal address is connected, the **PayPal** section shows **Payouts go here** and you can't change the address yourself. Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com, and we'll help you change it.

## Option 2: Bank account

Bank payouts are available in the US and 100+ other countries. Bank payouts can take 2 to 3 weeks longer to arrive than PayPal, so PayPal is the fastest way to get paid.

1. Under **Bank account**, click **Set up a bank account**.
2. Under **Where is your bank account?**, choose the country your bank is in. This can differ from your citizenship or tax status, and it can't be changed after setup.
3. Follow the steps to verify your identity and add your bank details.

Your bank setup shows one of these statuses:

| Status | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Setup in progress** | Finish the remaining steps. |
| **Verification pending** | Your details are being reviewed. Nothing to do. |
| **Action required** | More information is needed before we can send a payout. Open the bank setup and complete it. |
| **Ready for bank payouts** | You're set. |
| **Payout setup unavailable** | Contact us so we can review your payout options. |

We pay in US dollars. If your bank account is in another currency, the amount is converted and deposited in your local currency. To change your bank details later, contact support.

If your country isn't in the list, or bank setup isn't available for your account yet, contact us about other options.

## Which method is used

If you have both PayPal and a bank account set up, **payouts go to PayPal** unless you ask us to switch them to your bank. To switch, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

## After your payout method

You also need an approved tax form before we can pay you. See [Tax forms: W-9 and W-8BEN](/earnings-and-payouts/tax-forms-w-9-and-w-8ben). When everything is done, the checklist shows **Ready for the next payout**.
