Stores pay for the streams and uses of your music, and we turn those payments into a monthly royalty statement. Your share of each statement is added to your balance on the **Money** page, and once you're set up to be paid, we pay your balance out automatically.

## From a stream to your balance

1. **Fans stream or use your music** on Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, YouTube and the other stores.
2. **Stores report and pay.** Stores report earnings monthly, usually a month or two after the streams happen.
3. **We publish a statement** for each month. It covers every store and every track.
4. **Your share is calculated** for each track:
   * The label's or platform's share comes off first. Your percentage is set in your agreement with us.
   * If the track has an advance or costs to recoup, the recoupment is applied. See *Advances and recoupment*.
   * If you split the track with collaborators, each person's percentage of the artist share goes to them. See *Split royalties with collaborators*.
5. **Your balance goes up** by your share, and the month appears under **Earnings by statement**.
6. **We pay you** on a schedule once your balance reaches $50 and you've set up a payout method and tax form. See *When do I get paid?*

## Where to see your earnings

* **Money** shows your **Available balance**, your **Earnings by statement** for the last 3 months, and **Recent transactions**. Click **View all** for the full list.
* **Analytics → Earnings** shows your own earnings from your statements, alongside your other analytics.
* **Download statement** on the Money page gives you a spreadsheet with every line, store and country. See *Download and read your royalty statement*.

## What the balance labels mean

* **Available**: the amount ready to be paid in the next payout.
* **Pending**: an estimate from a statement paid in a foreign currency. It becomes available once the payment clears. See *Statements paid in another currency*.

## Why earnings and streams don't line up

Stream counts in **Analytics** are daily and a few days behind. Earnings come from monthly statements and are a month or two behind. So this month's streams show up in earnings later. Different stores and countries also pay different amounts per stream. See *Why don't my streams match my earnings?*

## Common questions

### What share of my royalties do I keep?
It's set in your agreement with AMM Distro. Your statement shows the **Label cut %** on each track. If you're not sure, ask your manager, or send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

### Do I need to request my money?
No. There's nothing to request. Payouts happen automatically once you're set up.

### I'm a collaborator on someone else's track. How do I get paid?
Accept the split invitation, then set up a payout method and tax form. See *Accept a royalty split invitation*.
