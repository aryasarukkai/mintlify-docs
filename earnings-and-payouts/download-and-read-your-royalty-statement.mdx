You can download your royalty statement from the **Money** page as an Excel spreadsheet. It shows what you earned on every track, from every store and country, with the label's share and any splits already applied.

## Download a statement

* **All your earnings:** click **Download statement** at the top of the Money page.
* **One month:** under **Earnings by statement**, click the spreadsheet icon next to the month.

![The Royalties & Payments header with the Download Statement button](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-money-download-statement.webp)

![Earnings by Statement with the download icon for one month marked](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-money-balance-statements.webp)

The file opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

## The Summary sheet

The top of the **Summary** sheet shows:

| Row | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Recipient** | Your name. |
| **Period** | The month or months the statement covers. |
| **Currency** | Always USD. |
| **Tracks** | How many of your tracks earned in this period. |
| **Available balance** | Earnings ready to be paid out. |
| **Pending (estimate, awaiting payment)** | Estimated earnings from statements paid in a foreign currency. See *Statements paid in another currency*. |
| **Already paid out** | Earnings we've already paid you. |
| **Total earnings** | All of the above added together. |

Below that is one row per track:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Track**, **Artist**, **Release**, **ISRC**, **Period** | Which recording and which month. |
| **Streams** | Streams reported for the track in that period. |
| **Track revenue** | What the stores paid for the track. |
| **Label cut %** | The label's or platform's share, as set in your agreement. |
| **Artist share** | What's left for the artist after the label cut. |
| **Your royalty split %** | Your percentage of the artist share. It's 100% unless the track is split with collaborators. |
| **Your earnings** | What you earned from the track. |
| **Status** | Whether it's available, already paid, or pending. |

## The Detailed sheet

The **Detailed** sheet has one row per store report line: **Store**, **Territory** (country), **Sale date**, **Type**, **Units**, **Store revenue**, **Rate per unit**, **Your earnings** and **Your rate per unit**, along with the track's **UPC** and **ISRC**.

Use it to see which stores and countries your money comes from. You can sort and filter it like any spreadsheet.

## Common questions

### Why is my per-stream rate different between stores and countries?
Each store pays a different amount, and the same store pays different amounts in different countries and for free and paid listeners.

### My statement doesn't match my stream counts in Analytics.
Analytics is daily and a few days behind; statements are monthly and a month or two behind. See *Why don't my streams match my earnings?*

### Something on my statement looks wrong.
Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com, with the month, the track and what you expected to see.
