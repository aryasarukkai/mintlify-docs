title: "I can't find my release in a store"
description: "Most of the time a missing release is still being processed by the store, isn't out yet because of its release date, or isn't available in your country."
---

Most of the time a missing release is still being processed by the store, isn't out yet because of its release date, or isn't available in your country. Start with the release's **Delivery** tab, which shows where each store is up to.

## 1. Check the release status

Open **Releases** and click the release. The status at the top tells you where it is:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **In review** | We're still reviewing it. Review usually takes 1–2 business days. |
| **Approved** / **Delivering** | It's being prepared and sent to the stores. |
| **Delivered** | It's in the stores and goes live on the release date. |
| **Live** | It's available on streaming platforms. |
| **On hold** | Delivery is paused while we fix a problem. We'll contact you if we need anything. |

If the release date is in the future, the stores have it but won't show it to fans until that date.

## 2. Check the store on the Delivery tab

Open the release and click the **Delivery** tab. It lists each store with its own status, such as **Pending**, **Delivered** or **Live**, and an estimate of how long the store needs to process it.

![The Delivery tab showing counts of live, delivered and pending stores, with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and TIDAL listed](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-delivery-tab-store-status.webp)

The tab gives these typical times after delivery, and notes that "Processing times are estimates and vary by platform":

* Most stores: about 15–60 minutes.
* Spotify: about 45–90 minutes.
* YouTube Music: 3–10 business days.

Click **Refresh** to load the latest status.

## 3. Check your stores and countries

* **Stores:** if you picked specific stores when you built the release, it only goes to those. The **Stores** card in **Review & submit** shows what you chose.
* **Countries:** if you blocked some countries, or released only in some, the release won't appear in stores in the other countries. A release can look missing because you're browsing from a country it isn't released in.

## 4. Search the right way

* Search for the **release title and the artist name** together, not just the title.
* Open the artist's page in the app and look under releases. A new release can take longer to show on the artist page than in search.
* If you find it by searching but it's not on your artist page, it may be on another artist's page. See *My release is on the wrong artist page*.

## Still missing?

If a store shows **Live** or **Delivered** on the Delivery tab, the release date has passed, and you still can't find it after the time above, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com. Include the release name, the store, and the country you're searching from.

## Common questions

### It's on Spotify but not on another store.
Each store processes releases on its own schedule. Check that store on the **Delivery** tab.

### My song is on Instagram or TikTok but I can't add it to a video.
Social platforms are a separate choice in the builder. See *Your music on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube*.
