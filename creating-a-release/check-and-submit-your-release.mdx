Before you can submit, every **Preflight** check must pass, you must answer any **Spotify profile protection** question, and you must make your **Content ID & social** choice. Then click **Submit for review** at the bottom of the builder, confirm the release date, and click **Confirm and submit**.

## Open Preflight

1. In the builder, scroll to **Review & submit**.
2. The **Preflight** card shows how many things are still to fix, for example "2 things still to fix", or "Everything checks out. You'll confirm the release date next." when you're ready.
3. Click the card to see the list.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-preflight-checklist.webp" alt="The Preflight dialog showing one item that must be fixed before submitting" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

The list has two parts:

* **Must be fixed**: these block submission.
* **Worth a look**: warnings. You can submit with these, but check them.

Preflight updates as you edit. Fix an item, and it disappears from the list.

## How to fix each check

### Release details

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Release title is required" | Add a title in **Release**. |
| "At least one primary artist is required" | Add an artist under **Artists**. |
| "A release can list up to 4 primary artists…" | Move the extra artists to featured artists. |
| "Artwork is required" | Upload a cover. See **Artwork requirements**. |
| "Release date is required" | Pick a date, or choose **Release as soon as possible**. |
| "Primary genre is required" | Pick a **Genre**. |
| "Copyright (C) line is required" / "Phonogram (P) line is required" | Fill in **Record label**. |
| "…needs a legal first and last name before this can be submitted" | Click the artist and add their legal first and last name. |
| "…has no Spotify profile linked" (warning) | Link the artist's Spotify profile if they have one. If it's their first release, you can leave it. |
| "Single-track releases should have the track title match the release title" (warning) | Use the same title for the release and the track. |

### Tracks

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| "At least one track is required" | Upload audio in **Tracks**. |
| "Track N: title is required" | Give the track a title. |
| "…is missing its audio file" | Upload the audio for that track. |
| "…needs its existing ISRC because it was previously released" | Enter the ISRC, or switch the track to **New recording**. |
| "…is missing a songwriter", "…a performer credit", "…a production credit" | Add the missing credit. See [Add songwriter, producer and performer credits](/creating-a-release/add-songwriter-producer-and-performer-credits). |
| "…is missing a lyricist (Lyricist, Songwriter or Composer & Lyricist)." | The track has vocals and lyrics, so credit whoever wrote the lyrics. If the track is instrumental, answer **No, it's instrumental** under **Vocals**. |
| "…has no role assigned" | Pick a role for that person. |
| "…says "remix" in its title or version but no one is credited as Remixer" | Credit the remixer. |
| "…will be delivered as "Name Name"…" (warning) | Add the writer's full legal first and last name. |

## Spotify profile protection

The first time we deliver to an artist's Spotify profile, **Review & submit** asks: **Does this profile have Artist Profile Protection turned on?** Preflight shows "Confirm Spotify profile protection for … in Review & submit." until you answer.

![The Spotify profile protection question with Yes selected, Give us the artist key, and the key field](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-spotify-profile-protection.webp)

* **No**: click **No**, then **Confirm protection is off**.
* **Yes**: choose one:
  * **Give us the artist key**: paste the key from Spotify for Artists → Settings → Artist Profile Protection and click **Save key**. It looks like `XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`.
  * **I'll approve manually**: you'll approve the release in Spotify for Artists yourself. Until you do, it may be delayed or not appear on the profile.

If you're not sure, open Spotify for Artists → Settings and check.

## Submit

When everything passes, click **Submit for review**. A final step opens:

1. **Release date**: choose **On a specific date** and pick a day, or **As soon as possible**. With **As soon as possible**, the release date is set to the day we approve the release.
2. **Summary**: check the title, artists, tracks, release date, stores and **Content ID & social media** choice.
3. **How it's delivered**: says when the release goes live. With a specific date, it goes live at midnight local time in each region.
4. **Warnings**: anything worth checking. These don't stop the release from being submitted.
5. Click **Confirm and submit**.

You'll see "Submitted for review" and we'll email you once it has been reviewed. Review usually takes 1–2 business days.

After you submit, the builder is locked. If we ask for changes, it opens again.

### If you're on a label's roster

If your account is a sub-account under a label, your release goes to the label first and shows **Label review**. The label can edit it and then send it on to us.

### If you have an unsigned contract

If your account has a contract waiting for your signature, you'll see "Your account has a pending contract that must be signed before submitting releases." Open **Agreements**, sign it, and then submit.

### Transfers without ISRCs

If you're moving a release from another distributor and some tracks have no ISRC, you'll be warned that tracks are missing ISRCs. Add them if you have them, so streams stay linked. You can still submit without them.

## Common questions

### The Submit for review button is greyed out.

Open Preflight and fix everything under **Must be fixed**, answer any Spotify profile protection question, and make your **Content ID & social** choice.

### Can I edit my release after I submit it?

Not while it's in review. If something's wrong, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com, and we can send it back to you.
