You can release covers, tracks with samples and remixes, as long as you have the rights to what you use. In the builder, mark a cover or interpolation as **Cover** and link the original song so its writers are credited. Samples must be cleared. Remixes must credit the remixer.

## Cover songs and interpolations

A track counts as a **cover** if it contains the composition or lyrics of another song. That includes a full cover, an interpolation (re-singing or re-playing part of another song) and a sampled melody or lyric.

1. Open the track in the builder and scroll to **Credits**.
2. Switch **Original** to **Cover**.
3. Paste the original song's Spotify link and click **Find**.

![The Credits area with Cover selected and the field for the original song's Spotify link](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-builder-cover-switch.webp)

This brings in the original song's songwriters so the composition credits are right, and tells our review team which song your track uses. Check the imported writers, then add your own performers and producers.

Write the title the way you want it to appear. Don't add the original artist's name to your track title or artist list unless they actually performed on your recording.

## Samples

Samples are fine. **Uncleared samples aren't.** Before you release a track with a sample, get permission from whoever owns the sampled recording (and the song, if you use its melody or lyrics).

If the sample uses another song's melody or lyrics, also mark the track as **Cover** and link the original, as above.

If you don't hold exclusive rights to every sample in the track, don't send it to Content ID and social platforms. Uncleared samples get claims reversed and can put your account at risk. See **Content ID and social platforms**.

Your manager can help with sample clearance.

## Remixes

* Put the remix name in the track title, in brackets: *Greedy (Ian Asher Remix)*.
* Credit the remixer with the **Remixer** role. A track with "remix" in its title or version can't be submitted without one.
* You need permission from the owner of the original recording to release a remix of it.

If you can't add the remixer credit in the builder, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

## Beats you bought or leased

If you made a song on a beat from a producer or beat store, check your licence before you release:

* The licence must allow distribution to streaming services.
* A **non-exclusive** licence usually means other artists can use the same beat. Don't opt in to Content ID and social platforms for that track, because their releases would match yours.

Credit the beat's producer under **Production**.

## Why this matters

Releases with uncleared samples, unlicensed covers or remixes, or wrong rights claims can be sent back or rejected, and repeated problems can stop us accepting releases from an account. Ask your manager if you're unsure.

## Common questions

### Do I need to credit the original songwriters on a cover?

Yes. That's what the **Cover** switch is for. Link the original and its writers are added as composition credits.

### Can I release a sped-up or slowed version of my own song?

Yes. Add it as a new track with **Version** set to *Sped Up* or *Slowed*. It's a new recording, so it gets its own ISRC.

### Someone claimed my track on YouTube or filed a copyright claim.

Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com. If you receive a copyright claim in AMM Distro, you can respond to it from the claims page in the app.
