By default, your release goes to every store we deliver to, in every country. You can change both in the **Review & submit** section of the builder: **Stores** to pick specific stores, and **Territories** to block countries or release only in some.

For most releases, leave both as they are.

## Pick specific stores

1. Open your release in the builder and scroll to **Review & submit**.
2. Click the **All stores** card.
3. Choose **Pick stores**. (**Everywhere** sends the release to every store.)
4. Use **Search stores…** to find a store, and click stores to turn them on or off. **Select all** and **Clear** change them all at once.
5. Click **Save**.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-stores-picker.webp" alt="The Stores dialog with Everywhere, Pick stores and the store search highlighted" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

The card then shows how many stores you picked, for example "12 stores – A custom selection."

TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Content ID and SoundCloud monetisation are chosen separately, in **Content ID & social**. See **Content ID and social platforms**.

## Block or limit countries

1. In **Review & submit**, click the **Worldwide** card.
2. Choose one of:
   * **Worldwide**: every country we deliver to.
   * **Remove countries**: everywhere except the countries you pick.
   * **Only add countries**: nowhere except the countries you pick.
3. Search for a country, or use a **Quick add** group such as **European Union**, **North America**, **UK & Ireland**, **DACH**, **Nordics** or **Australia & NZ**.
4. Click **Save**.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-territories.webp" alt="The Territories dialog with Worldwide, Remove countries and Only add countries" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

The card then shows, for example, "Delivering everywhere except 3 countries" or "Delivering to 5 countries only".

## When to change these

* **Stores**: if you have an exclusive deal with a store, or there's a store you don't want your music on.
* **Countries**: if someone else already holds the rights to the song in some countries, or a deal limits where you can release it.

## Common questions

### Which stores do you deliver to?

We deliver to 44 stores and platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, TIDAL, Deezer, Pandora, iHeartRadio, SoundCloud, Audiomack, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Content ID and Snapchat, regional services in Asia, Africa, the Middle East and Russia, and services for fitness, venues and music recognition. The **Stores** dialog shows the current list for your release.

### Can I add stores after my release is out?

Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

### My release isn't on one store.

Some stores take longer to process new releases than others. Open the release's **Delivery** tab to see the status for each store. If it's been longer than the estimate shown there, contact us.
