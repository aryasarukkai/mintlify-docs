Stores are strict about how titles and artist names are written. This article explains where each piece of information goes in the builder so your release shows correctly and isn't sent back.

## Release title and version line

* **Title**: the name of the release, exactly as it should appear in stores.
* **Version line**: only for releases that are a version of something, such as "Remixes", "Versions" or "Deluxe". Leave it empty for a normal release.

On a one-track **single**, the release title has to match the track title. If they differ, the builder shows "A single's release title has to match its track." with a button to use the track's title. After that, the two stay in sync.

## Track title and version

Open a track to see its **Title** and **Version** fields.

* A **remix** or a **named artist** goes in the title, in brackets: *Greedy (Ian Asher Remix)*, *Song (feat. Artist)*.
* Anything else that describes the version goes in **Version**: *Sped Up*, *Slowed*, *Radio Edit*, *Acoustic*, *Instrumental*.

![An open track with the Version field highlighted](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-builder-track-details.webp)

### Remixes

If a track's title or version contains the word "remix", the remixer must be credited with the **Remixer** role. Preflight flags a remix with no remixer, and you can't submit until it's fixed. If you can't add the remixer credit, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

## Artists

**Artists** in the Release section is everyone billed on the release, up to 4 primary artists. Type a name in **Search or add an artist…**, and pick the artist or create a new one.

When you create an artist, you're asked for:

* Their **Spotify** profile (search or paste a link) and **Apple Music** profile, if they have them.
* Their legal **First name** and **Last name**. This stays private and is needed before you can submit.

If the Spotify profile you pick has a different name, you'll be asked to confirm it's the right artist. If the artist doesn't have a Spotify profile yet, click **Not on Spotify yet? Create without a profile**. Their Spotify page is created when their first release goes live. Preflight will remind you that the artist has no Spotify profile linked, which is fine for a first release.

A release can list up to **4 primary artists**. Stores show a release with more than 4 as "Various Artists", which takes it off every artist's page, so the builder won't let you submit with more. Add the extra names as featured artists instead.

## Featured artists

Click **Add featured artists** to add someone as a feature.

* **On a single**, a featured artist is billed on the release itself.
* **On an EP or album**, featured artists on a track are credited on that track. They get the credit and the streams, but the release doesn't appear on their artist page.

Listing someone as a main artist instead costs nothing extra and puts the release on their profile, which helps their exposure. Only use **Featured** when you don't want the release on their page.

On a single, the track's artists always match the release.

## Metadata language

Set **Metadata language** in the Release section to the language your titles are written in. The default is English.

## Common questions

### My release went to the wrong artist profile.

Make sure each artist's Spotify and Apple Music profiles are linked in the builder before you submit. After a release has been delivered, the profile links are locked so our records keep matching the stores. If a link is wrong, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

### How do I credit two artists who perform together as a duo?

If the duo has its own artist profile, add the duo as one artist. If they release under their own names, add each person as a separate artist.

### Can I use ALL CAPS or emoji in titles?

Write titles the way they should appear in stores. Our team checks formatting during review and will ask for changes if a store is likely to reject it.
