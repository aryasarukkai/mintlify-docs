TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Content ID and SoundCloud can recognise your recording in other people's videos and uploads and pay you when it's used. You choose whether each release goes to them in **Content ID & social** in the builder. You must make this choice before you can submit.

Only opt in if you hold the rights to **everything** in the track, including every sample.

## Make your choice

1. Open your release in the builder and scroll to **Review & submit**.
2. Click the **Content ID & social** card. Until you choose, it shows **Needs a decision**.
3. Choose **Distribute to UGC** or **Don't distribute**.

If you choose **Distribute to UGC**:

4. Tick **I hold exclusive rights to this recording and every sample in it.**
5. Under **Choose the stores**, tick the platforms you want.
6. If you picked YouTube Content ID, tick the extra confirmation: **I own the exclusive rights to this content and I want it delivered to YouTube Content ID.**
7. Click **Save**.

## What each platform does

| Platform | What happens |
|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Your audio becomes a sound others can use in their videos. Paid per video. |
| **Meta — Instagram & Facebook** | Claims ad earnings on Instagram and Facebook videos using your audio. It also adds your track to Meta's in-app Audio Library. |
| **YouTube Content ID** | Scans all of YouTube and earns from videos that match your recording. It's the strictest of the four. |
| **SoundCloud** | Monetises your track on SoundCloud and pays out on plays. |

## When not to opt in

Choose **Don't distribute**, or leave a platform unticked, if:

* The track uses a **sample you haven't cleared**, or a beat you licensed without exclusive rights.
* The track is a **cover** or uses parts of another song you don't control.
* Someone else already claims this recording on these platforms.
* The recording uses **public domain** or **royalty-free** material that other people can also use.

Uncleared samples get claims reversed and can put your account at risk. On YouTube, a wrong claim is reversed and counts against the account.

## Common questions

### My song is on Instagram but people can't find it.

Your track has to be delivered with **Meta — Instagram & Facebook** turned on to appear in the in-app audio library. Check the release's **Content ID & social** choice. If it was off, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

### A viral video uses an edit of my song, but it's not linked to my release.

Use the **TikTok** tab on your released release to claim the sound. Paste the TikTok sound or video link, and we'll check whether it matches your recording.

### My own YouTube video got a Content ID claim from my release.

That's Content ID matching your own upload. Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com, with the video link and we'll help.

### Can I change my choice after release?

Contact us. Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.
