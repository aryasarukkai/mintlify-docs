After your release is approved, you can follow it to each store from the release page. The status at the top shows where the release is overall, and the **Delivery** tab shows each store on its own.

## Where to look

1. Open **Releases** and click the release.
2. The **Status** card on the **Overview** tab shows the overall status. Click **View delivery** to jump to the store list.
3. Or click the **Delivery** tab. It appears once the release has been queued for delivery.

## Release statuses after approval

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | "Approved and being prepared for delivery." |
| **Awaiting signature** | Sign the release agreement to continue. Delivery starts after it's signed. |
| **Delivering** | Being sent to stores. This usually takes 2–3 business days. |
| **Delivered** | The release is in the stores and goes live on the release date. |
| **Live** | "Available in stores." |
| **On hold** | "Delivery is paused while we fix a problem. We'll contact you if we need anything." |

The release moves from **Delivering** to **Delivered** or **Live** as soon as the first store has it, so some stores may still be processing it after the status changes.

## The Delivery tab

The **Platform Delivery Status** panel shows how many stores have the release, and a card for each store.

![The Delivery tab with the Refresh button and the All Platforms filter numbered, and cards for Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and TIDAL](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-delivery-tab-store-status.webp)

* **Live**: the store is showing the release to fans.
* **Delivered**: the store has the release and is processing it, or it goes live on the release date.
* **Delivering** (or **Preparing**): the release is on its way to that store.
* **Pending**: the release hasn't reached that store yet.
* **Issues**: something went wrong with that store. We'll fix it and contact you if we need anything.

Use the filters (**All Platforms**, **In Progress**, **Delivered**, and **Live**, or **Ready At Release** before the release date) to narrow the list, and **Refresh** to load the latest status.

### How long stores take

The tab shows an estimate for each store. Typical times after delivery:

* Most stores: about 15–60 minutes.
* Spotify: up to 24 hours.
* YouTube Music: usually 2–10 minutes.

Some stores take several business days to process a release. Processing times are estimates and vary by platform.

## Notifications

With **Release updates** turned on in **Settings → Notifications**, we email you about release approvals, rejections and changes needed. The AMM Distro mobile app shows the same statuses and can send push notifications.

## Common questions

### My release says Live, but I can't find it on a store.
Check that store's card on the **Delivery** tab. See [I can't find my release in a store](/stores-and-artist-profiles/i-cant-find-my-release-in-a-store).

### My release is On hold.
You don't need to do anything unless we contact you. If it's been on hold for a while, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

### My release is stuck on Awaiting signature.
Open the release and sign the agreement. If collaborators also need to sign, delivery starts once everyone has signed.
