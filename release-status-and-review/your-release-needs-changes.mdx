If our team finds something to fix during review, your release moves to **Revision requested** and we email you. The release page shows our notes in a **Revision requested** box at the top. Make the changes in the builder and submit it again.

## Find the notes

1. Open **Releases** and click the release marked **Revision requested**. Home also shows it in the **Updates** card.
2. Read the **Revision requested** box at the top of the page. It shows the date it was reviewed and lists each thing that needs to change.

![A release with a Revisions Requested box, the Edit Release button and the Revisions Needed status card](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-revisions-requested.webp)

## Make the changes and resubmit

1. Click **Edit release** in the **Revision requested** box, or click **Fix** next to one item to go straight to that field in the builder.
2. Make each change the notes ask for.
3. Scroll to **Review & submit**, check that **Preflight** passes, click **Submit for review**, then **Confirm and submit**.

The release goes back to **In review**. Review usually takes 1–2 business days.

## Common reasons we ask for changes

* **Artwork** that shows a website address, social handle or contact details. Stores reject these.
* **Titles** that don't follow the rules: a remix or feature that isn't in brackets in the title, or version information in the wrong field. See [Titles, versions and featured artists](/creating-a-release/titles-versions-and-featured-artists).
* **Credits** that are missing or incomplete, such as a writer without a legal first and last name.
* **Artist profiles** linked to the wrong Spotify or Apple Music page.
* **Cover songs** or samples that aren't marked as covers or linked to the original.

The notes always say exactly what to change. If you don't understand a note, ask us.

## If the date is close

Fix and resubmit as soon as you can. The release still has to pass review and be delivered before your release date. If you need a new date, change it in the builder while you're editing.

## Common questions

### I don't agree with the change you asked for.

Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com. Your manager can talk it through with you.

### What's the difference between Revision requested and Rejected?

**Revision requested** means the release can go out once you fix the notes. **Rejected** means we won't distribute it. See [Why was my release rejected?](/release-status-and-review/why-was-my-release-rejected)
