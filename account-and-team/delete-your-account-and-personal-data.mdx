To have your personal data deleted and your account closed, go to **Settings**, find **Delete my data** and click **Request deletion**. We complete requests within 30 days, and you can withdraw the request until then. The request covers both AMM Distro and ANTHM, because they use the same account.

## Before you ask

* **Get paid first.** Once your details are deleted, we can't send you earnings. If you have a balance, make sure it's paid out before you ask.
* **Hand over accounts you own.** If other people use an account you own, make one of them the owner so they keep access.
* **Download what you need.** Save your royalty statements and agreement PDFs. You won't be able to sign in afterwards.
* **Decide about your releases.** Releases in stores stay up. If you want them removed, ask for a takedown first.

## Send the request

1. Go to **Settings**. **Delete my data** is on the first page.
2. Click **Request deletion**.
3. Read what's deleted and what's kept. Add anything we should know under **Anything we should know? (optional)**.
4. Click **Send request**.

We email you to confirm we received the request, and again when it's done. The **Delete my data** section shows the date you asked and the date we'll complete it by.

To cancel, click **Withdraw request** in the same section before we complete it.

<Info>
Can't sign in, or acting for someone else? Email team@ammdistro.com from the address on the account, with "Delete my data" in the subject. If you're acting for someone else, include proof that you're authorized to.
</Info>

## What's deleted

* Your name, email address, profile photo and contact details. Your account is replaced by an anonymous placeholder.
* Your sign-in sessions, devices, push notification tokens and connected apps.
* Your notifications, email preferences and AI assistant conversations.
* Your PayPal email and the profile details you use for marketing tools.
* Your access to every account you belong to. Pending invitations sent to your email are cancelled.

## What's kept

* **Signed agreements.** Distribution, release and split agreements you signed stay in force under their own terms. Deleting your data doesn't cancel them.
* **Tax records**, for as long as tax law requires, generally 7 years.
* **Earnings and payment records**, for as long as accounting law requires, generally 7 years. Your collaborators' records of what they were paid from shared releases stay too.
* **Releases** already delivered to stores, and their public credits.

The full list is on the [What we delete and keep](https://ammdistro.com/legal/data-deletion) page.

## Common questions

### Will my music come down from the stores?
No. Releases in stores stay up. Ask for a takedown before or with your request if you want them removed. See [Take a release down](/after-release/take-a-release-down).

### Can I keep my account and only delete some data?
To change your name or email, see [Change your name, email or notification settings](/account-and-team/change-your-name-email-or-notifications). For anything else, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.
