Label accounts can show their own name, logo and colour on the dashboard and on the sign-in, invitation and notification emails their team and artists receive. You set it up under **Settings** → **Label branding**. The dashboard shows "Powered by AMM" under your label name.

Branding is part of Label Plus and is turned on per account.

<Info>
**Label branding** only appears in Settings on label accounts. Only admins of the account can change it.
</Info>

## Ask for branding

If branding isn't on for your account, the page says "Branding is not on for this account".

1. Go to **Settings** and open **Label branding**.
2. Under **Anything we should know (optional)**, add any details, for example how many artists you work with.
3. Click **Request branding**.

We'll reply by email.

## Set up your branding

Once branding is on:

1. Go to **Settings** and open **Label branding**.
2. Under **Name and colour**:
   * **Label name**: shown at the top of the menu, in the browser tab and on emails.
   * **Colour**: used for buttons, the selected menu item, links and the first chart series. Pick a swatch or enter a hex value, like #4d8dff.
   * **Theme**: light or dark. Light suits most logos. Dark matches the standard AMM Distro look.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Under **Logo and mark**, upload your images. Each can be PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 5 MB.
   * **Logo**: your wide wordmark. It appears at the top of emails, and in the menu when the theme is dark.
   * **Mark**: a square icon. It appears next to your name in the menu, in the account list and as the browser tab icon.

The **Preview** section shows how the menu looks to your team and artists.

## Who sees your branding

* Everyone who uses your label account.
* Artists on your label's sub-accounts. Their dashboard and emails show your label's branding.
* People you invite to your account, on their invitation email.

## Common questions

### My logo doesn't show in the menu.
The wide **Logo** shows in the menu only when the theme is dark. With the light theme, the menu shows your **Mark** next to your label name.

### I don't see Label branding in Settings.
The account you're in isn't a label account. Use the account switcher to change to your label account. See [Switch between accounts](/account-and-team/switch-between-accounts).
