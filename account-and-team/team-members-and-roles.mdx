You can give other people access to your AMM Distro account, such as a manager, a bandmate or someone at your label. Each person signs in with their own email and gets one of three roles: **Admin**, **Member** or **Viewer**.

## What each role can do

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access: manage team, releases, royalties, and account settings. |
| **Member** | Can create and edit releases, view analytics, but cannot manage team or billing. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to releases and analytics. Cannot make any changes. |

The person who created the account is its owner. Only Admins can invite people, change roles or remove someone. Only Admins can request a takedown or an edit to a release that's already in stores.

## Invite someone

1. Go to **Settings** and open **Team members**.
2. Click **Invite member**.

![The Team members page with the Invite member button](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-settings-team.webp)

3. Enter their **Email Address**. **Name (Optional)** is only for your reference.
4. Pick a **Role**: Admin, Member or Viewer.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-team-invite-dialog.webp" alt="The Invite Team Member dialog with email, name and role" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

We email them a link to accept. If they don't have an AMM Distro login yet, the link creates one. They need to sign in with the same email address you invited.

<Info>
Invitations expire after 7 days. If someone misses it, invite them again.
</Info>

## Change a role or remove someone

1. On **Team members**, click the **...** menu next to the person.
2. Under **Change Role**, pick **Admin**, **Member** or **Viewer**. The change applies straight away.
3. To take away their access, choose **Remove from team** and confirm.

![The menu for a team member with Change Role and Remove from team](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-team-change-role.webp)

A removed person loses access to this account immediately. You can invite them again later. You can't remove yourself.

## Common questions

### The invitation says "Email does not match invitation"
The person is signing in with a different email from the one you invited. Ask them to use the invited address, or send a new invitation to the email they use.

### I see "Pending invitation already exists for this email"
That person already has an invitation waiting. Ask them to check their inbox and spam folder.

### I see "User is already a member of this account"
They already have access. Change their role from the **...** menu instead.

### Can a team member see my earnings?
Admins have full access to the account. Members and Viewers can see releases and analytics. Give the Admin role only to people you trust with your money and settings.

### Do team members get paid royalties?
No. Being on the team gives access to the account. To pay someone a share of a track, set up a royalty split on the release.
