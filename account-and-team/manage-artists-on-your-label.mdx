If you run a label account, you can bring artists under your label as sub-accounts. Each artist keeps their own login and builds their own releases, and every release they submit comes to you for review before it reaches our team.

<Info>
Sub-accounts are only available on label accounts. If you don't see **Sub-Accounts** in Settings, the account you're in isn't a label account.
</Info>

## How sub-accounts work

The **Sub-Accounts** page sums it up: "Sub-accounts are artist accounts that release under your label. Each artist keeps their own login and can manage their releases, but you'll have oversight of their catalog. Releases from sub-accounts require your approval before submission. Royalties flow through your label account."

## Add an artist to your label

You need the Admin role on the label account.

1. Go to **Settings** and open **Sub-Accounts**.
2. Click **Add Artist**.

![The Sub-Accounts page with the Add Artist button and one active artist](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-settings-sub-accounts.webp)

3. Enter the **Artist Email**. Optionally add an **Artist/Project Name**.
4. Click **Send Invitation**.

The artist gets an email to accept. Until they do, they're counted as **Pending** on the page. Once they accept, they show as **Active**.

## Review an artist's release (Label review)

When a sub-account artist clicks **Submit for review**, the release goes to **Label review** first. The artist sees "Waiting for your label to approve it."

To review it:

1. Open the release. Its status shows **Label review**.
2. Click **Review and edit**. The release opens in **Label Review Mode**, which says who submitted it.
3. Check everything, and fix anything that needs it. You can edit any metadata and add or remove tracks.
4. Click **Review & Approve**, check the summary, and confirm.

You'll see "Release approved and forwarded for admin review!" The release then moves to **In review** with our team, the same as any other release. The usual review checks still apply.

<Info>
**Review and edit** opens the classic release editor, which looks different from the one-page builder your artists use. Your changes are saved to the same release.
</Info>

## Open an artist's account

On **Sub-Accounts**, use the menu next to an artist and choose **View Account** to switch into their account. Use the account card at the top of the sidebar to switch back.

## Remove an artist from your label

1. On **Sub-Accounts**, open the menu next to the artist.
2. Choose **Remove from label** and confirm.

The artist keeps their own account, but their releases will need to be transferred. Contact support before you remove an artist with live releases so we can sort out where those releases and their royalties should sit.

## Common questions

### Can I add someone who already has an AMM Distro account?
Yes. Use the email they sign in with. They accept the invitation from the email we send.

### Where do the artist's royalties go?
Royalties from sub-account releases flow through your label account. How they're shared with the artist is set by your agreement with them. If you're not sure how a release is set up, contact support.

### What's the difference between a sub-account and a team member?
A team member works inside your label's account. A sub-account is the artist's own account, with its own releases, under your label.
