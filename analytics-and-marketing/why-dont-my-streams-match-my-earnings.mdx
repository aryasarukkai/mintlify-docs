title: "Why don't my streams match my earnings?"
description: "Your stream counts and your earnings come from different reports on different schedules, so they rarely line up for the same days."
---

Your stream counts and your earnings come from different reports on different schedules, so they rarely line up for the same days. Streams are reported daily, a few days behind. Earnings come from monthly royalty statements, usually a month or two after the streams they pay for.

## The two clocks

| | Streams | Earnings |
|---|---|---|
| **Where you see them** | **Analytics** (Overview, Consumption, Engagement, Geography) | **Analytics → Earnings** and **Money** |
| **How often** | Daily | Monthly, by statement |
| **How far behind** | A few days | A month or two after the streams |

So the streams you see this month are usually paid on a later statement. When you compare, match a statement month with the streams from the period it covers, not with this week's chart.

The Earnings tab says which statement month it's "reported through".

## Why the totals differ even for the same period

* **Not every play is paid.** Plays that stop before 30 seconds aren't paid streams. Analytics shows them as **Sub-30s**.
* **Each play pays a different amount.** What a stream earns depends on the store, the listener's country and whether they're on a free or paid plan. **Consumption → Monetization** shows the mix when stores report it, and **Earnings** shows your average rate per 1,000 streams.
* **Stores filter their own counts.** Streams are counted after the platforms' own fraud filtering.
* **Your statement shows your share.** Statements already have platform fees, the label's share and royalty splits applied. "Your earnings" is what's left for you.
* **Advances are recovered first.** If your release has an advance or costs to recoup, the release's **Recoupment** card shows how much of your share goes toward it.
* **Some money is still pending.** Earnings paid in another currency show as **Pending** on the **Money** page until the payment clears and the final rate is locked in.
* **Spotify for Artists counts differently.** The **Spotify** tab shows Spotify's own counts, which won't match store reports. Your earnings are based on store reports.
* **Video use is paid differently.** **UGC views** count views of videos using your music. They're paid by the platforms under their own rules, not like streams.

## Check what you were paid for a track

1. Go to **Money**.
2. Under **Earnings by statement**, download the statement for the month.
3. Open the **Detailed** sheet. Each row shows the track, store, territory, units, store revenue, rate per unit and your earnings.

## Common questions

### My streams went up but my earnings didn't
The new streams are probably not on a finalized statement yet. Check again after the next one or two statements.

### My earnings show a store that isn't in my analytics
Not every store sends daily stream data. A store that only reports on statements appears in **Earnings** and in your statements, but not in the daily charts.

### Can you tell me exactly how much a stream pays?
No fixed rate exists. It changes by store, country, plan type and month. Your statements show the actual rate per unit you received.
