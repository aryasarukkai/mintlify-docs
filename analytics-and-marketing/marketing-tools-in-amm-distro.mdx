The **Marketing** page in AMM Distro has tools for promoting a release: a social post maker, a press kit builder and your smart links. They use your release artwork and details, so you don't need design software.

To open them, click **Marketing** in the sidebar. Each tool marked **Available** is ready to use.

![The Marketing tools page with Social Media Kit, Press Kit / EPK and Promo Links](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-marketing-tools.webp)

## Social Media Kit

Make social posts from your release artwork.

1. Click **Social Media Kit**.
2. Under **Select Release**, pick a release.
3. Pick a **Format**:
   * **Square** (1080x1080)
   * **Circle** (1080x1080)
   * **Story** (1080x1920)
   * **Banner** (1500x500)
   * **YouTube** (1280x720)
4. Pick a **Text Overlay**: **Out Now**, **Coming Soon**, **Available Now**, **New Release**, **Pre-Save Now**, **Custom Text**, or **None / Custom**. Choose its **Position**: **Top**, **Center** or **Bottom**.
5. Turn the **Logo Watermark** on or off. It adds a small "AMM Distro" mark.
6. Check the **Preview** and click **Download PNG**.

![The Social Media Kit with a release selected, the format and text options, and Download PNG](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-social-media-kit.webp)

## Press Kit / EPK

Create a press kit with your bio, music and links, and download it as a PDF.

1. Click **Press Kit / EPK**.
2. Under **Artist Info**, fill in **Artist Name** (required), **Bio** (up to 500 characters) and **Genre Tags**.
3. Under **Releases**, choose the releases to feature.
4. Under **Social Links**, add your Spotify, Apple Music, Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok links. Under **Contact**, add a contact email and your management or label name.
5. Check the **Preview**, then click **Download PDF**.

<Warning>
The press kit isn't saved in AMM Distro. Download the PDF before you leave the page.
</Warning>

Tips from the page: keep your bio to 2–3 paragraphs, choose a few releases to feature, and include links to your music.

Your smart links: one page per release that sends fans to every store, with pre-saves before release day. See **Smart links for your releases**.

## Creator campaigns and more on ANTHM

ANTHM is our marketing platform for music. You can use it to run creator campaigns on TikTok and other platforms, track how your sound is used, and build link-in-bio pages. Open it from **ANTHM** at the bottom of the sidebar. You use the same login. Using ANTHM is optional.
