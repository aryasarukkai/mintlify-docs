Every release gets a free smart link: one anthm.link page that lists your release on every store, with a Spotify pre-save before release day. We create it for you when your release is approved, so most of the time there's nothing to set up.

## Find your links

Go to **Marketing** in the sidebar and open **Promo Links**. Each release's link is listed with its URL, its status and its views and clicks.

![The Promo Links page with the New smart link button and the Copy button on a link](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-smart-links-list.webp)

* Click **Copy** to copy the link, or **Open** to see the page as fans see it.
* Click **Analytics** to see visitors, store clicks and where they came from.

## Before and after release day

Before release day, the page leads with a Spotify pre-save and lists the other stores as coming soon. Once the release is out, the store buttons fill in as each store's link is found. If a link shows "No store links found yet", it's still waiting on the stores and checks again on its own. Click **Refresh** to check now.

## Make your own link

Click **New smart link**, or use the **+** button at the top of the sidebar and choose **Smart link**. You can make one from a release or from a UPC.

## Change the URL

You can change an automatic link's URL once. Click the pencil next to the URL. The old URL stops working, so change it before you share the link widely.

For the full guide to smart link pages, pre-saves, themes and link analytics, see **Smart links and pre-saves** in the ANTHM section.
