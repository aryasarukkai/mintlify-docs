When you sign in, you land on **Home**. It shows your balance, your streams, your latest releases and anything new. The left menu takes you everywhere else.

![The AMM Distro Home page with the main areas numbered](https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-dashboard-tour.webp)

## 1. Account switcher

The card at the top of the left menu shows the account you're working in, such as your artist name or your label. If you belong to more than one account (for example your own and a label's), click the card and pick another account. Everything you see changes to that account.

## 2. The + menu

Click **+** to start something new:

* **New release**: a single, EP or album.
* **Music video**: upload a video for one of your songs. If it's greyed out, music videos aren't turned on for your account.
* **Import a release**: a release that's already out elsewhere.
* **Smart link**: one link to your release on every store.

## 3. Search

Click **Search** or press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows) to jump to any release, artist or page. You can search releases by title, artist, UPC or Release ID.

## 4. Main menu

| Item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your overview. |
| **Releases** | Every release on the account, with its status. Search by title, artist, UPC or Release ID, and filter by status. Music videos are a tab on this page. |
| **Agreements** | Release agreements and collaborator agreements, sorted into **Awaiting your signature**, **Signed** and **Archived**. |
| **Analytics** | Streams and listeners from the stores, plus a separate **Spotify** tab with Spotify for Artists data, and an **Earnings** tab. |
| **Money** | Your balance, statements, payout method, tax form and transactions. |

Below that, **Account** has **Notifications**, **Settings** and **Help center**, and **Tools** has **Tools** (including Catalog Transfer) and **Marketing**.

## 5. Recent releases

Your latest releases with a short line under each. Before a release is approved, the line is its status, such as "Draft", "In review" or "Revision requested". Once it's approved, the line is its date, such as "Out Nov 14" or "Released Jun 19". Click one to open it, or click **Recent releases** to see them all.

## 6. ANTHM

Opens ANTHM, our marketing platform for creator campaigns. It uses the same sign-in. You don't have to use it. Label accounts don't show this link.

## Home

Above the cards are **Search** and the **New** button, which opens the same menu as **+**.

* **Balance**: what you've earned. Click the eye icon to show or hide it, and **View earnings** to open **Money**.
* **Streams**: streams over the last 30 days with a small graph. Click **Open analytics** for more. Store data is a few days behind.
* **Support**: **Open support chat** to message our team, or **Help center** for guides.
* **Latest releases**: your three most recent releases. Click **See all** for the full list.
* **Updates**: things that need you or news about your releases, such as a release that needs changes or a signature, a split invitation, project files your label asked for, a copyright claim, or a playlist add. Click **See all** for everything.

If you're the owner of a new account, you may also see **Finish setting up payouts**. Click **Set up payouts** (or **Continue setup**) to add a payout method so you can be paid.

## Common questions

### Why are there no streams on Home?

Until there are numbers, the card says **No streams yet**. Numbers appear once a release is live and the stores have reported. Store reports arrive daily, a few days behind.

### I can't see a release I know exists.

Check the account switcher. The release may be on another account you belong to.

### Some menu items are missing.

What you see depends on your role. Viewers can't make changes, and accounts that only receive royalty splits see **Home**, **Releases**, **Analytics** and **Money** for the releases they have a split on.
