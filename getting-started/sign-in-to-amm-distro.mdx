AMM Distro has no passwords. You sign in with a passkey or with a link we email you. It's the same account you use for ANTHM, so if you're already signed in there, you may go straight through. Use the email address your invitation was sent to.

## Sign in with an email link

1. Go to ammdistro.com and click **Client Login**. You'll see "Taking you to sign in…" and then **Sign in to AMM Distro.**
2. Enter your **Email address** and click **Continue with email**.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-sign-in.webp" alt="The Sign in to AMM Distro page with the email field and the Send login link button" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

3. The screen changes to **Check your inbox** and says "Waiting for you to open the link". Leave it open.
4. Open the email from AMM Distro and click the link. The link works once and lasts 15 minutes. You can open it on any device, for example your phone.
5. The first screen continues on its own, signs you in and takes you to AMM Distro.

## Your sign-in number

Under **On another device?**, the **Check your inbox** screen shows a two-digit number.

<img src="https://ammdistro.com/helpdesk/distro-sign-in-check-inbox.webp" alt="The Check your inbox screen showing a two-digit sign-in number" style={{ maxWidth: "800px" }} />

* If you open the link in the **same browser**, you're signed in straight away.
* If you open the link on **another device or browser**, you'll be asked to pick a number. Pick the one shown on the screen where you started.

If the wrong number is picked, the sign-in is blocked and nobody is signed in. Send a new link and try again. This check stops someone else from using a link you didn't request.

## Sign in with a passkey

After you sign in with an email link, you may be asked to **Add a passkey**. A passkey lets you sign in with Face ID, Touch ID or your device PIN instead of waiting for an email. It works for AMM Distro and ANTHM, and you can still sign in with an email link. Click **Not now** to skip.

Next time, click **Continue with passkey** (or **Sign in with a passkey**) on the sign-in page.

## No email arrived

* **Check your spam or junk folder**, or search your inbox for "AMM".
* **Check the address.** If you mistyped it, click **Use a different email**.
* **Use the email you were invited with.** If you have more than one email, try the one your invitation went to.
* **Send a new one.** After a short countdown you can click **Resend link**. Only the newest link works.

If you still don't receive anything, send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.

## Error messages

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "That sign-in took too long or was already used. Try again." | Click **Try again** and send a new link. |
| "There's no AMM Distro account for that sign-in. Use the email your invitation was sent to." | Sign in with the email address your invitation went to. |
| "We couldn't keep you signed in on this browser. Check that cookies are allowed, then try again." | Allow cookies for ammdistro.com, then click **Try again**. |
| "This account is locked. Contact support for help." | Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com. |
| **Sign-in didn't finish** | Click **Try again**, or **Sign in with an email link instead**. |

## How long you stay signed in

On the web you stay signed in for 7 days. In the mobile apps you stay signed in for 60 days. To sign out, click your name at the bottom of the left menu and choose **Sign out**.

## Sign in on the mobile app

1. Open the AMM Distro app and tap **Continue with AMM**.
2. Sign in on the page that opens, with a passkey or an email link, the same way as on the web.

You can also tap **Sign in with a code instead**, enter your email and tap **Email me a code**. We email you a 6-digit code. Enter it in the app, or tap **Open AMM Distro** in the email. Codes last 15 minutes. After 5 wrong tries you need a new code, and you can request up to 5 codes for one email address every 15 minutes. If you see "Too many sign-in codes requested for this address", wait a few minutes and try again.

## Common questions

### Can I change the email I sign in with?

Not yourself. Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com, and we'll change it for you.

### Can I sign up without an invitation?

No. AMM Distro is invite-only. Apply from ammdistro.com, or ask the person who manages your team to invite you.

### It says my access has been suspended.

Send us a message from the chat on this page, or email team@ammdistro.com.
